Request Account Deletion

Atlanta Ayyappa Seva Sangam (AAYSSA)

Delete your account and personal data

Use this page to request deletion of your AAYSSA membership portal account and personal information associated with it. You do not need to be signed in.

A membership is maintained as a household record. If family members share the same account, AAYSSA will confirm whether the request applies to the entire household or only the requesting person's information before deleting data.

Information covered by the request

  • Membership profile and contact information
  • Family-member details, preferences, and volunteer interests
  • Program registrations, Home Pooja requests, and related portal records
  • Authentication access associated with the membership account

Records that AAYSSA must retain for legal, financial, fraud-prevention, or nonprofit reporting obligations may be kept only for the required period. For example, donation transaction records may need to be retained even after portal access and other personal data are deleted.

Submit your request

Enter enough information for AAYSSA to locate the account. Selecting the button opens a prepared email to the AAYSSA team. Send the email from an address registered on the account whenever possible.

What happens next

AAYSSA will verify the request using the registered contact information before deleting data. The team will confirm when the request is complete or explain any information that must be retained.

If the button does not open your email application, email aayssa.official@gmail.com with the subject “AAYSSA Account Deletion Request.”
