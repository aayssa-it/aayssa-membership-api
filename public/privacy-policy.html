Privacy Policy

Atlanta Ayyappa Seva Sangam (AAYSSA)

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how Atlanta Ayyappa Seva Sangam (AAYSSA) collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information through its membership portal, public forms, and related community services.

Information we collect

Depending on the services you use, AAYSSA may collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, mailing addresses, and household or family-member information
  • Membership details, communication preferences, volunteer interests, and seva participation
  • Program registrations, attendance information, Home Pooja requests, event responses, and feedback
  • Donation-related records, such as donor identity, campaign, amount, and transaction reference; payment-card details are handled by the payment provider and are not stored in the AAYSSA portal
  • Authentication and technical information needed to provide and protect the service, such as sign-in email, session data, IP address, browser information, and service logs
  • Photos or other content that you voluntarily provide for a community activity

How we use information

  • Provide and administer memberships, programs, events, donations, volunteering, and community services
  • Authenticate members and maintain account security
  • Communicate confirmations, reminders, updates, and responses to requests
  • Coordinate activities with authorized AAYSSA volunteers and administrators
  • Maintain operational, financial, safety, and nonprofit records
  • Improve the portal, troubleshoot problems, prevent misuse, and protect AAYSSA and its community

How information is shared

AAYSSA does not sell personal information. Information may be shared:

  • With authorized AAYSSA board members, administrators, and volunteers who need it to provide a requested service
  • With service providers supporting hosting, databases, authentication, email delivery, donations and payments, and related operations
  • When you direct or consent to sharing, such as participating in a public recognition activity
  • When required by law, legal process, financial reporting obligations, or to protect rights, safety, and service integrity

Service providers are permitted to process information only for their contracted services and under their own applicable privacy and security obligations.

Family and children's information

The portal organizes membership information by household and may include details about children supplied by a parent or guardian. Parents and guardians should provide only information needed for AAYSSA services and may request that a child's information be corrected or deleted, subject to required record retention.

Cookies, sessions, and external services

The portal uses essential browser storage and session technologies to keep members signed in and protect authenticated areas. Links or embedded services operated by other organizations are governed by those organizations' privacy policies.

Data retention and security

AAYSSA retains personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including membership administration, financial and nonprofit reporting, dispute resolution, security, and legal compliance. Retention periods vary by record type.

AAYSSA uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so members should also protect access to their registered email accounts.

Your choices and requests

You may review or update available profile information through the member portal. You may also contact AAYSSA to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, or to ask a privacy question. AAYSSA may verify your identity and household authority before completing a request.

To request deletion of your portal account and associated data, visit the Account Deletion Request page.

Changes to this policy

AAYSSA may update this policy as its services or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest published version.

Contact AAYSSA

For privacy questions or requests, email aayssa.official@gmail.com.
